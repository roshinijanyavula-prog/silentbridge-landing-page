One bridge. More ways to connect.

SilentBridge explores how AI-enabled tools could support two-way communication—without wearables or specialized hardware.

Sign to text & speech

Explore camera-based recognition of selected Indian Sign Language (ISL) signs, with recognized messages presented as text and intended speech output.

Speech to text

Help hearing users' spoken words appear as text, supporting conversations where listening to speech may not be accessible.

Simple, inclusive design

A clear, approachable interface built around the goal of more independent communication in everyday situations.

How the experience could work

A simple concept flow focused on making communication feel more natural.

STEP 01

Choose how to communicate

Use a camera-based sign input or enter spoken words through a speech-to-text flow.

STEP 02

Convert the message

AI-assisted recognition and language processing are intended to turn supported input into an accessible message.

STEP 03

Share and understand

Display the message as text and, where supported, provide speech output to help both people take part.

Technology with a human purpose

Our mission is to help reduce communication barriers and encourage more inclusive education, healthcare, workplaces and public services. SilentBridge is a prototype concept; recognition accuracy and supported signs must be tested with Deaf users and sign-language experts.
